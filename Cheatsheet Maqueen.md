# Cheatsheet Maqueen

Ga naar:

```text
https://makecode.microbit.org/#
```

Login in (with your Betabit account, any Microbit will work)
Click “New Project”
Enter a name for the project
Click   and then “Extensions”
Search for “maqueen”
And select maqueen
Now you are ready to start.

For the first time testing, you need to pair the microbit with the makecode environment

- Click the … next to download
- Connect device
- Plug in the usb cable
- Click Next
- Click Pair
- Select the usb device in the popup and click connect

With download you can put the program on the microbit.
Then disconnect the USB cable and turn on the maqueen with the switch at the back.
